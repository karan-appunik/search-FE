Questions

Good to know

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Install the app and switch it on in your theme settings. Everything else happens automatically.

Will my store look different?

No. Askmerce works inside the search bar your theme already has, so your design stays as it is.

What happens when I change products?

Search updates automatically. New products, edits and stock changes are picked up on their own.

Can I choose what shows first?

Yes. Goals & rules let you boost, lower or hide products in search results.
